Steady OG selective reuse

The owner supplied the local "steady og" Android project and requested its
behaviors, components and scenes in this personal build. The audited archive
SHA-256 is d1ddd3bfa67b6e05884cfbb7126fb946f245df1e4f218c78342b3dbfe19ad290.

Reused sources:
- ui/break_timer/audio/AmbientSoundEngine.kt: mono sound-generation algorithms,
  adapted into PcmSoundGenerator and the lifecycle-safe AudioSoundscapeEngine.
- res/drawable/img_nature_portal.xml, img_rain_portal.xml,
  img_campfire_portal.xml: offline vector scene assets.

The supplied project contains no LICENSE or NOTICE. This records provenance
and personal owner-authorized reuse; it does not invent an open-source licence
or establish permission for third-party redistribution. See the repository's
docs/steady-og-source-inventory.csv for exact source paths and SHA-256 hashes.

Other OG workflows are adapted into the existing destination storage, timer,
theme and platform contracts. No physiological defaults, fake sensor values,
CDN scripts, provider keys or private Safety export behavior are inherited.
