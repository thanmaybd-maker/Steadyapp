## Implementation Details
- **LogicalDayPolicy**: Implemented to switch day boundaries at 04:00 (parameterized by `boundaryMinutes`).

- **TodayScreen**: Compose UI for editing the plan, with TalkBack semantic descriptions and large controls. Integrates a button to open the Food reference.
- **FoodIdeasSheet**: Bottom sheet displaying `StaticFoodReference` templates.
- **Navigation**: Updated `SteadyAppNavigation` to route "Today" to `TodayScreen` injected with `TodayViewModel`.
